## Origin
Angels are forged from stars or angelic reproduction.
### Angelic Reproduction
Two feathers are taken from each participant's wings and are left in a specialized container for several months. Sitar Herself has to bless the container for the child to survive.'
### Successor Star
Every few hundred years, Sitar carefully selects *two* stars, rather than just one, and personally raises an angel to be Her successor. She also selects at most two other angels to help discipline and guard this angel. In the most recent case, [[Perseus]] was the successor and [[Raym]] was the guardian, however both have been banished.
## Beliefs
Sitar personally designed the angelic school curriculum, so angel children are raised to believe in Her ways. These include:
* Each angel is blessed with a soulmate born within the same ten minutes.
* So long as you follow Sitar's ways, you will feel fulfilled in life.
### Justice System and Indoctrination
While lenient towards children, the [[City of Angels]] justice system is often ruthless. Crimes are included but not limited to:
* Swearing excessively
* Asking for help when they are able to do the task on their own.
